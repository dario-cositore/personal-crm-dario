# Outreach cadence and compliance

## The cadence

Touch 1 (day 0): first touch, drafted as a file, approved in a batch, sent.
Touch 2 (day +3): follow-up in the same thread.
Touch 3 (day +7): second follow-up.
Touch 4 (day +14): final follow-up.
After touch 4 with no reply: move the client to the stale list for nurture.

## Compliance footer (written into every draft before review)

- Reason for contact: public listing / professional interest.
- Opt-out line: "Reply 'stop' and I won't contact you again."
- No invented claims. Only what is on their own site.

## Rules that never bend

- Nothing sends without batch approval.
- An unapproved row is refused with a reason, never a silent success.
- Drafts live as files under ~/.ai-enablement-agent/pipeline/<date>/.
- The cadence is drafted by the heartbeat, approved by you, sent by command.
- Disposition: yes -> production. no or silence -> stale list.

## Writing the first touch

- Subject: short, specific, references their business by name.
- Body: 4-6 sentences. What you noticed, what you do, one concrete outcome, a low-friction next step.
- Never "I hope this email finds you well". Never "just checking in".
- One ask: a 15-minute call or a reply with one sentence.
