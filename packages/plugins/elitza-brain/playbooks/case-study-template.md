# Case study template

## Title

[Client name] — [one-line outcome, e.g. "Cut intake time from 4 hours to 20 minutes"]

## Client

- Name and trade.
- Size: employees, locations.
- Region.

## The problem (before)

- What they did by hand.
- Hours per week.
- What broke and how often.
- What they had tried before.

## What we built

- Bullet 1: concrete artifact.
- Bullet 2: concrete artifact.
- Bullet 3: concrete artifact.

## The result (after, 30 days)

- Hours saved per week: X -> Y.
- Error rate: X% -> Y%.
- Response time: X hours -> Y minutes.
- Conversion: X% -> Y%.

## Quote

"[One sentence from the owner, in their words.]"

## Timeline and price (optional, with permission)

- Sprint length: X days.
- Price: EUR X.

## Permission

Always get written permission before publishing. Offer to anonymise if they prefer.
