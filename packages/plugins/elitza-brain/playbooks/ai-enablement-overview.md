# AI Enablement — overview

AI Enablement is the practice of helping a local business turn repetitive, document-heavy, or decision-heavy work into a small, reliable automated system, then handing it over so they own it. You are not selling "AI". You are selling a finished, scoped outcome with a price and a handover.

## The one-line value proposition

"I take the work you do by hand every week — intake, triage, follow-up, document handling — and turn it into a system that runs without you, in a week, for a fixed price, and I hand you the keys."

## Who buys

- Local businesses with 2-20 staff, a real owner, and a recurring manual process that costs hours every week.
- Trades, professional services, healthcare-adjacent, real estate, agencies, logistics, accounting, legal.
- Someone who has tried a spreadsheet or a SaaS and is frustrated, but is not technical.

## Who does not buy

- Solo operators with no process to automate.
- Enterprises with IT departments (they build in-house).
- Anyone who wants "AI" as a buzzword with no scoped outcome.

## The loop you run

Scout -> qualify -> first touch -> cadence (4 touches) -> yes/no -> diagnose -> proposal -> workbook -> scheduled -> payment -> done -> handover.

Every step is a tool. Only one step (outreach) reaches a human, and only after batch approval.

## Pricing anchors

- Single flow: EUR 1,500 (2 days)
- Two or three flows with a CRM: EUR 2,500 (3 days)
- Document processing with retention and access rules: EUR 3,500 (4 days)

Fixed scope, fixed price. No hourly rates. No features not in the diagnosis note.
