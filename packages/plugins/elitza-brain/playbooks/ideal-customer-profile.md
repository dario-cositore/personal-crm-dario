# Ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Signals of a good client

- 2-20 employees, owner-operated, revenue EUR 200k-2M.
- A process that repeats weekly: leads arriving, documents flowing, follow-ups depending on someone remembering.
- They already pay for 2-4 SaaS tools and complain about them.
- A visible owner who answers the phone or email personally.
- A website that exists but is not a corporate machine.
- Google reviews mentioning "slow", "disorganised", "lost paperwork", "had to call twice".

## Signals to walk away

- No owner, only a manager with no budget authority.
- "We'll figure it out as we go" — refuse time and materials.
- Several owners who disagree and won't get on one call.
- Wants a custom app for what is a configuration.
- Existing competitor contract ending soon and they want you to undercut — propose now, start after.

## Scoring (0-100)

- Has a website with a real contact email: +20
- Phone answered by a human (not a menu): +15
- Rating 4.0+ with 20+ reviews: +15
- Category matches a known playbook (trades, professional services, healthcare-adjacent): +20
- Employee count 2-20: +15
- Has a visible owner name: +10
- Has a documented manual process (forms, spreadsheets, email chains): +5

Below 40: do not scout. 40-60: scout, low priority. 60+: qualify and first touch.
