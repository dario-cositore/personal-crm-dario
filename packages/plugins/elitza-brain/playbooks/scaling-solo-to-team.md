# Scaling from solo to team

## Solo (EUR 0-10k/month)

- You do everything: scout, outreach, diagnose, build, deliver, handover.
- Tools: Elitza, n8n/Make, a CRM (this one), Resend.
- Focus: repeatable verticals, reusable workbooks, 2-4 sprints/month.

## First hire (EUR 10-25k/month)

- Hire a builder/deliverer, not a salesperson. You keep sales.
- Document your playbooks so they can run them.
- Hand over the workbooks and the delivery SOP.
- You review every proposal and handover.

## Small team (EUR 25-60k/month)

- Split: scout/outreach, delivery, support.
- Standardise the workbook library.
- Add a part-time support retainer.
- Track metrics per person.

## Rules

- Do not hire until you have 3 months of consistent revenue.
- Do not hire a salesperson first. The founder sells best early.
- Document before you delegate. A playbook is a hire enabler.
- Keep the fixed-scope model. Hourly billing does not scale.
