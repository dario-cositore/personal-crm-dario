# Pricing models

## The default: fixed-scope sprint

- Single flow: EUR 1,500 (2 days)
- Two or three flows with a CRM: EUR 2,500 (3 days)
- Document processing with retention and access rules: EUR 3,500 (4 days)

Fixed scope, fixed price. No hourly rates. No features not in the diagnosis note.

## Retainer (after the sprint)

- Monthly support: EUR 300-800/month for 2-5 hours of tweaks, monitoring, and small additions.
- Sold only after a successful sprint. Never upfront.

## Value-based (when the numbers are big)

- If the process costs them EUR 5,000/month in labour, price at 10-20% of annual savings.
- Requires a quantified diagnosis. Do not guess.

## ROI calculation (use in proposals)

- Hours saved per week x hourly cost x 52 = annual savings.
- Payback period = price / (annual savings / 12).
- Target payback under 3 months.

## Rules

- Never discount. Add scope or remove scope instead.
- Never quote hourly. It invites scope creep.
- Always state payment terms (50% upfront, 50% on delivery, or full on delivery for small jobs).
- Always include an out-of-scope list. That is what protects the price.
