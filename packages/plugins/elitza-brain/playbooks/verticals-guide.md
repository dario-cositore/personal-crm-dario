# Verticals guide

## Trades (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, builders)

- Pain: missed calls, quote follow-up, scheduling, invoicing.
- First flow: intake qualification (phone/web form -> CRM -> quote).
- Second flow: scheduled follow-up on open quotes.
- Workbook: intake qualification (2 days, EUR 1,500).

## Professional services (accountants, lawyers, consultants)

- Pain: document intake, client onboarding, deadline tracking.
- First flow: document intake with OCR and filing.
- Second flow: client status board with next action.
- Workbook: zero-leak document processing (4 days, EUR 3,500).

## Healthcare-adjacent (clinics, dentists, physio)

- Pain: appointment booking, no-shows, patient intake forms.
- First flow: intake form -> CRM -> reminder sequence.
- Constraints: GDPR, patient data, sector rules. Keep PII minimal.
- Workbook: CRM automation (3 days, EUR 2,500).

## Real estate

- Pain: lead triage, viewing scheduling, document collection.
- First flow: lead qualification + routing.
- Second flow: viewing scheduler with reminders.

## Agencies

- Pain: client reporting, brief intake, asset collection.
- First flow: brief intake -> task board.
- Second flow: status reporting to clients.

## Adapting a vertical

1. Scout with trade-specific queries.
2. Score with trade-specific weights.
3. Use the trade's vocabulary in outreach.
4. Pick the matching workbook.
5. Adjust the env-var checklist (sector-specific compliance).
