# Metrics and ROI

## Before/after measurement

Measure before the sprint and 30 days after:

- Hours per week on the process (manual).
- Error rate (re-typed fields, lost documents).
- Response time (lead to first reply).
- Conversion rate (lead to booked job).
- Number of tools used.

## KPIs to track per client

- Leads captured per week.
- Leads qualified (scored) per week.
- Follow-ups sent on time (should be 100%).
- Documents processed without manual touch.
- Hours saved per week.

## Case study template

- Client: name, trade, size.
- Before: the manual process, hours, cost, errors.
- What we built: 2-3 bullets, concrete artifacts.
- After: numbers, hours saved, errors reduced.
- Quote: one sentence from the owner.
- Timeline and price (optional, with permission).

## Reporting to yourself

- Log every sprint: client, price, days, hours, outcome.
- Track conversion: scouted -> contacted -> yes -> done.
- Review monthly: which verticals convert, which playbooks get reused.
