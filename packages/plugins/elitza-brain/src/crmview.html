Cold Reachout

          
Mass cold-email reachout with mini-batches, buffer delays, and domain warmup.

        

        

          
Warmup & deliverability guidance

          

            Daily cap per mailbox (research-based): Stage 0 new domain 5-10/day (warmup only) · Stage 1 (wk1-2) 10-20 · Stage 2 (wk3-4) 20-30 · Stage 3 (mature) 30-50 · Stage 4 (fully warmed) 50-100 (use inbox rotation).

            Buffer between sends: random 2-5 min (120-300s) between emails to mimic human behavior. Never send at exact intervals.

            Spread: send within business hours (9am-5pm recipient TZ). Verify emails first (bounce <5%). Keep spam complaints <0.1%. Use a dedicated sending domain, not your primary. Ramp gradually over 2-4 weeks.

            Batching: split the full list into mini-batches (default 10). Process one batch per "Run due" click. Scaling beyond one mailbox = inbox rotation.
